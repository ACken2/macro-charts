## Whether a 10% decline reaches 30%

Eight indicators vote on whether a decline that has already reached 10% goes on to 30%.

Each indicator is one feature. Its cutoff is a depth-1 tree fit only on declines that were already over. A split needs three earlier declines on each side. A print older than 180 days is left blank. The vote calls 30% when more than half of the indicators with a print that day say so. An even split calls a recovery. A blank is left out of the count.

The eight features are the 12-month percent change in the 10-year minus 2-year yield, the 6-month change in WTI, the 6-month change in PCE inflation, the 6-month change in large-bank C&I standards, the 6-month change in wage inflation, the level of the St. Louis financial stress index, the 12-month change in small-firm C&I standards, and labor-force participation's gap versus its prior 36-month high.

A column clears the bar when both call groups contain at least four declines and the 30% calls reach 30% at least 20 percentage points more often than the recovery calls.
