Macro indicator charts

Self-contained Plotly pages. Each file is several megabytes; the first load can take a while. Research charts, not investment advice.

K-wave

  • Phase probabilities The leading phase only, plus the runner-up in a transition, and the five bucket z-scores.
  • Valuation structure Shiller CAPE, trailing P/E, and the split of 12-month returns into earnings and multiple.
  • Monetary, debt, and real rates 10-year yield, term premium, breakeven real rate, and federal interest over receipts.
  • Productivity, capex, and diffusion Fernald TFP, hyperscaler capex over free cash flow, and software revenue relative to that capex.
  • Commodity and relative ratios DBC over SPY, the long PPI analog, gold over copper, and SPY over the equal-weight ETF.
  • Returns by phase Real equity and long-bond returns. Since 1974 uses the rules model with one-year flickers removed. Since 1871 adds a judged history before that.

Business cycle

  • Cycle position Eight indicators vote on whether a 10% decline reaches 30%, marked on the S&P.
  • Lead study Which series turned 2 to 18 months before those crashes, and which did not.
